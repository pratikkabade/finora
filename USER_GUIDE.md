# 💰 Finora - Your Personal Finance Manager

Welcome to **Finora**! A simple yet powerful app to take control of your personal finances. Track every rupee (or penny) you spend and earn, visualize your spending patterns, and plan for recurring bills—all in one beautiful, easy-to-use interface.

## 📌 What is Finora?

Finora is your personal finance companion that helps you:

- 📝 Keep track of all your income and expenses

## 💡 Core Features Explained

### 1. Adding Transactions

A **transaction** is any movement of money - earning, spending, or moving between your accounts.

#### **Three Types of Transactions:**

**📥 Income** - Money coming in

- Salary from your job
- Freelance earnings
- Gift money
- Returns or refunds
- Any other money received

**📤 Expense** - Money going out

- Groceries
- Gas for your car
- Restaurant bills
- Subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, etc.)
- Anything you buy or pay for

**🔄 Transfer** - Moving money between your accounts

- From checking to savings
- From one credit card to another
- Moving money between banks

#### **How to Add a Transaction:**

1. Click the **"+ New"** or **"Add Transaction"** button
2. Select the **transaction type** (Income, Expense, or Transfer)
3. Choose the **account** (which of your banks/cards it's from)
4. Enter the **amount**
5. Pick a **category** (or create a new one):
   - Expenses: Food, Transport, Entertainment, Utilities, Shopping, etc.
   - Income: Salary, Freelance, Bonus, etc.
6. Add a **title** (e.g., "Starbucks coffee", "Monthly salary")
7. Add optional **description** (e.g., "With college friends")
8. Select the **date and time**
9. Click **Save** ✓

That transaction is now saved locally on your device. If you're logged in with Google, it will also backup to the cloud automatically!

#### **Editing & Deleting Transactions:**

- Click any transaction to **edit** it
- Remove it by clicking the **delete button** if you made a mistake

### 2. Managing Your Accounts

An **account** represents any place where you store money - bank accounts, credit cards, wallets, etc.

#### **Why Have Multiple Accounts?**

- **Checking Account** - Daily spending
- **Savings Account** - Long-term savings
- **Credit Card** - Monthly dues
- **Wallet** - Cash on hand
- **Emergency Fund** - Money set aside

#### **How to Set Up Accounts:**

1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Look for **"Accounts"** section
3. Click **"+ Add New Account"**
4. Enter account details:
   - **Name**: e.g., "My Checking Account"
   - **Currency**: INR, USD, EUR, etc.
   - **Choose an icon** and **color** (for easy identification)
5. Click **Save**

#### **Calculate Your Net Balance:**

In Settings, you can choose which accounts to include in your **Net Balance** (the big number on the dashboard):

- Include your bank and savings accounts ✓
- Exclude debt or credit card balances (optional)
- See exactly what you're worth in liquid money

### 5. Planned Payments - Never Miss a Bill Again

A **Planned Payment** is a recurring reminder for bills or regular income you expect.

#### **Common Examples:**

- **Netflix Subscription** - $9.99 every month
- **Monthly Rent** - $1,500 on the 1st of each month
- **Monthly Salary** - $5,000 on the 25th
- **Car Insurance** - $100 every month
- **Gym Membership** - $50 every month
- **Quarterly Tax Payment** - $2,500 every 3 months

#### **How to Create a Planned Payment:**

1. Go to **"Planned Payments"** page (from navigation menu)
2. Click **"+ Add Planned Payment"**
3. Enter the details:
   - **Title** - e.g., "Netflix Subscription"
   - **Amount** - e.g., $9.99
   - **Category** - e.g., "Entertainment" or "Subscriptions"
   - **Account** - Which account it will be deducted from
   - **Start Date** - When this payment starts
   - **Frequency**:
     - Daily (every day)
     - Weekly (every 7 days)
     - Monthly (same date each month)
     - Yearly (same date each year)
     - Custom (every X days/weeks/months)
4. Choose if it's **One-time** or **Recurring**
5. Click **Save**

#### **Managing Your Planned Payments:**

The **Planned Payments page** shows:

- **🔴 Overdue** (Red) - Bills you missed and should pay ASAP
- **🟠 Due Soon** (Orange) - Bills due in the next few days
- **⚫ Scheduled** (Gray) - Bills coming up later

**Actions you can take:**

- **Mark as Paid** - Record that you've paid this bill (creates a transaction automatically)
- **Skip This Payment** - Delay it to next month (without marking as paid)
- **Pay Early** - Move the payment forward
- **Edit** - Change the amount or frequency
- **Delete** - Remove the recurring payment

#### **Real-World Benefit:**

Instead of remembering every bill, Finora reminds you and even creates the transaction for you automatically when you mark it as paid!

### 7. Keeping Your Data Secure

#### **🔐 PIN Protection**

Set a 4-digit PIN code to protect your financial data:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"PIN Protection"**
3. Set a **4-digit code** (like 1234)
4. Confirm it
5. Now every time you open the app, you'll need to enter this PIN

**Security:** If someone tries to guess wrong 5 times, the app locks them out for 15 minutes.

#### **☁️ Cloud Backup**

If you **sign in with Google:**

- ✓ Your data is automatically saved to the cloud
- ✓ You can use Finora on multiple devices (phone, tablet, computer)
- ✓ If you uninstall the app, your data is safe in the cloud
- ✓ One-click restore if you lose your data

If you use as a **Guest:**

- ✓ Data stays only on this device (maximum privacy)
- ✓ No cloud backup
- ✓ If you clear browser data, data is gone (so be careful!)

#### **Manual Backup/Restore:**

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Look for **"Backup & Restore"**
3. Click **"Backup to Cloud"** to save a copy
4. Click **"Restore from Cloud"** to recover old data

## ❓ Common Questions

### **Q: Is my data safe?**

A: Yes! If using Google Sign-in, data is encrypted in Firebase (Google's secure database). If using Guest mode, data stays only on your device.

### **Q: Can I use Finora on multiple devices?**

A: Yes, if you sign in with Google. Your data syncs across all your devices.

### **Q: What if I lose my phone?**

A: If signed in with Google, sign in on a new phone and your data is restored. If Guest mode, data is only on that device.

### **Q: Can I delete old transactions?**

A: Yes! Click any transaction and select delete. You can undo recent deletes.

### **Q: Do I pay to use Finora?**

A: Finora is free to use with all features included!

### **Q: Can I edit a transaction after adding it?**

A: Yes! Click on any transaction and edit any field.

### **Q: What currencies does Finora support?**

A: All major currencies - INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, etc. You can set different currencies for different accounts.

### **Q: How detailed can I get with categories?**

A: Very! Create as many custom categories as you need, and add descriptions to transactions.

### **Q: Can I export my data?**

A: Yes! Export to PDF (formatted report) or Excel (raw data for further analysis).

## 🆘 Need Help?

### **Troubleshooting:**

**Problem: Data not syncing**

- Solution: Check internet connection, refresh the page, sign out and back in

**Problem: PIN locked for 15 minutes**

- Solution: Wait 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts

**Problem: Can't add transaction**

- Solution: Make sure you've created at least one account first

**Problem: Charts not showing**

- Solution: Make sure you have transactions in selected date range

**Problem: Export not working**

- Solution: Try a different export format or try a shorter date range
